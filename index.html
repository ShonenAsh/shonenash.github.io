Text Analyzer: Linguistic Profiles

Jan 2026 – Sep 2026

A web app that shows the linguistic profile of any text using Biber's linguistic framework, entirely in the browser. I fine-tuned ELECTRA-small for part-of-speech tagging and dependency parsing, quantized it, and run it with ONNX Runtime across a pool of Web Workers, analyzing a 3,000-word text in about a second. The tokenizer, lemmatizer and feature extraction are ports of spaCy and PyBiber.

AI v/s Humans: Investigating Language Models

Sep 2025 – Dec 2025

A statistical deep dive into the patterns in AI-generated text. I used unsupervised methods (clustering, type-token and part-of-speech distributions) to compare AI and human writing, and trained a classifier on Biber linguistic features that reached an F1 score of 0.95.
